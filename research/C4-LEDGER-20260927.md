## P0 architecture decision

The serial Amdahl bound is `S ≤ 1/(1−f+f/r)` only when the presumed component occupies a critical serial fraction `f`. Eliminating 90% of one component would require at least `f=0.2778` to save 25% total time before overhead. No C4 instrument measures that `f` for masked CPU operations. Ten graph waves or 90% parking positions do not set it.
