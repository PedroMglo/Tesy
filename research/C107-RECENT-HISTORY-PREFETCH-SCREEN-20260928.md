# C107 recent-router prefetch screen

- **Objective/base:** Ask whether the immediately preceding routed experts provide useful future expert prefetch targets for C105's decode. Base C105 result `a31b40c`, hash-checked trace `bb3ae9352749283f76ce12b65ea76801916396f87849d609169215fefdf4e40d`. No model run. Evidence INFERIDO from measured target events.
- **Method:** Segment 32 teacher-forced decode steps from layer-0 to layer-35 `DEMAND` events, four experts/layer/step, excluding the masked final-prefill layer-35 set. For each layer, predict the union of routed experts in the previous 1, 2, 4 or 8 decode steps. Count only next-step demands with state=0 (absent from the primary pool) as potentially useful prefetch targets; state=1/2 already resident/loading.
- **Result:** Previous 1/2/4-token unions covered 0 of 1191/1140/982 absent demands. Previous 8 covered 2 of 724 (0.276%). Repeating a routed expert does not imply an I/O miss; the primary pools retained recent experts. These policies do not justify a physical prefetch implementation for this workload. The oracle absent bytes are logical, not observed physical NVMe traffic.
- **Decision/limits:** `NO_GO_RECENT_HISTORY_PREFETCH_ON_C105_DECODE` for this exact 189+32 teacher-forced profile only. Prediction lead time, waste after a residency check, alternative predictors, free-generation routing, M3 and M4 remain NOT_RUN. C100 `NO_GO_CONFIRM` and other FAILs unchanged. Next: examine bounded overlap of exposed waits or a different decode mechanism; a predictor needs evidence that it anticipates *novel absent* experts.
