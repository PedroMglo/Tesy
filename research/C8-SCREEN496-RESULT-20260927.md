# C8 P8 preload 496-token screen

- Objective/base: test the previously frozen medium prefill hypothesis in two alternating fresh-process pairs with 496 prompt IDs and 32 identical teacher-forced IDs. Measurement commit `77d7ba869797e31731daed995ee4373c2f63d5cb`, tree `609a3323bd506eab6abeecdbc7f5967ab856dc34`, clean at preflight. The original model/backend, P8 placement, ub32, slots32, E18 and no-prefix condition remained fixed; only native preload activation changed.
- Evidence class: four bounded uninstrumented target-host processes with valid raw hashes, mapped libraries, environment, loader map, O_DIRECT, 33 full finite logits vectors, masks/positions, step clocks and telemetry. Every arm's 33 vectors were bitwise equal to the contemporary C7c P8 OFF control. OFF issued zero preloads; ON issued 7749 per run. No process was retried.
- Measured: paired median gain `100*(OFF−ON)/OFF` was +17.7129% completed prefill, +16.8768% T_work and +4.4300% aggregate TPOT. Per-pair work gains were +17.1071% and +16.6465%; prefill gains +17.6648% and +17.7611%. Decode gained +9.42% in one pair and regressed −0.56% in the other; no stable decode improvement claim follows. The source wave-wait counter fell roughly 81–82 s OFF to 54–55 s ON, a diagnostic consistent with the hypothesis but not an exclusive critical-path proof.
- Resources: GPU total peak 3900 MiB, RSS 14,963,060,736 B, cgroup 14,757,126,144 B, CPU 93.125 C, GPU 61 C, NVMe 58.85 C, swap zero; all below frozen guards. Post-run GPU returned to 12 MiB with driver 615.71.09 and no NVIDIA/Xid kernel event in the queried interval.
- Decision: `SCREEN496_GO` under the prospective median prefill ≥10%, both prefill pairs positive, median work ≥8% and median TPOT ≥−5% rules. This is an investment screen with n=2, not a confirmed gain. Run three new alternating 496+32 pairs under the prospective confirmation thresholds; exclude all screening pairs.
- Alternative/limits: queue contention or variation could erase a smaller effect, so confirmation is discriminating. `ready_on_arrival` does not uniquely identify successful prefetch; global NVMe counters and `/proc/PID/io` are not device-exclusive expert traffic. Teacher forcing is synthetic; API TTFT, quality, 8K and sustained session are NOT_RUN. The focused 60-test model-free suite passed before execution.
