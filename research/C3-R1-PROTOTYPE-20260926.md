The independent diagnostic reads all 128 experts' six tensor payloads and router tensors from canonical GGUF offsets, using a separate no-cache ggml graph on the matching CPU or GPU backend. It uses the captured FFN activation and logical IDs/weights for its FFN arm and recomputes router IDs/weights separately from the canonical router tensors. MXFP4, F32 biases and GPT-OSS SwiGLU operator are unchanged. The comparisons above are exact bytewise equality of float32 outputs/weights and ordered top-k IDs; no numeric tolerance was needed. They condition on captured upstream activations. Slot-encoded bytes and slot generations have **not** yet been compared directly, so the integrity/lifetime axis remains open.

The first prototype does not clear R3: byte identity of consumer slots, masking/parking and generation lifetime are untested; only two layers, one case and four invocations are covered; capture OFF/ON full-model parity and negative mutants are NOT_RUN. No optimization candidate was launched. Next discriminating gate: capture slot bytes and wave IDs/masks at the actual consumer boundary, compare canonical GGUF slices and cover at least another prefill/decode state per device before expanding to all layers.
