# C3 broad callback OFF/ON logits protocol

Frozen before OFF runs. The ON arms are completed broad target captures `c3-r2-broad-log01` and `c3-r2-broad-spec01`, made with the exact C2 token-ID file and GPU8/32slots/ubatch32/no-preload configuration. The OFF arms use the existing pinned `c2_logits_probe_streaming` without a graph callback, with the same model, IDs, context4096, batch256/ubatch32, threads8, I/O4, direct I/O and no repack/op offload. Both arms use fresh processes/scopes; page-cache conditions are reported separately and are irrelevant to the numerical contract.

For `log_medium` expect 6 prompt rows and 43 continuation rows. Compare full 201,088-float32 vectors at prompt positions 31, 159 and 188, and continuation positions 189, 190, 196 and 220, mapped respectively to ON phases `prefill0`, `prefill128`, `prefill_final`, `decode0`, `decode1`, `decode7`, `decode31`. For `spec_pressure` expect 13 prompt rows and 49 continuation rows, comparing prompt 31, 159, 413 and continuation 414, 415, 421, 445. Every vector must be finite and **BITWISE_EQUAL**; all rows and their exact positions must exist once, with complete file lengths and no resource/backend failure. No tolerance. An OFF or ON run failure remains FAIL; no partial rows may be promoted. Full-vector parity at these 14 positions is additional to the independent per-layer FFN differential, not a full-model plain reference.

Resource envelope: unique cgroup memory.max18 GiB, swap.max0, RSS<=17 GiB, GPU<=7000 MiB, MemAvailable>=6 GiB, CPU/GPU/NVMe<=95/80/70 C; timeout300 s per OFF arm. Raw stays local and results are no-replace. The comparator writes source/analyzer hashes and rejects incomplete/duplicate row indices or non-finite values.
