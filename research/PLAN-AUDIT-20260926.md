Keep M2 partial, M6 advantage absent, extended-prefix resource failure and CLI timeout visible. There is no `STRONG_SUCCESS`, `EXCEPTIONAL_SUCCESS` or `HARD_NO_GO` evidence. The next discriminating scientific work, if separately authorized and budgeted later, is per-layer/tier instrumented traffic and broader prospectively fixed quality tasks; neither can be inferred from these results. Publication of this branch is the remaining administrative gate for the present request.
