# C83 — captured router footprint

- Objective/base: test whether the existing C76b numeric capture is sufficient to justify RAM L2 investment. Base and analysis measurement commit `2460cc1ea41e95036cada72fe8e3f4453f537a80`; C76b backend `27d2e42d8c994507ee6d71acc7c58d3eddd3f7a5`. Evidence class INFERIDO from hash-verified MEDIDO_NO_TARGET captures, with parser controls REPRODUZIDO_MODEL_FREE.
- Alternative: four checkpoints might constrain reuse enough to estimate L2 savings. They do not. Each decode checkpoint has 144 distinct layer/expert keys (1815.38 MiB), and their union has 394 keys (4967.08 MiB). Decode0→decode1 has 74 common and 70 newly observed keys. But four top-4 checkpoints need at most 16 keys per layer, below 32 current slots; missing intervening tokens and slot state can change every hit/miss inference.
- Decision: no L2 construction or performance claim from C83. Capture bounded continuous per-expert demand, resident/loading/miss status, eviction and I/O intervals, with request/phase markers, then simulate equal-memory policies against observed demand. The new instrumentation will have a separate identity and cannot promote its timing without a neutral bridge.

Full continuous decode trace, physical NVMe attribution, exposed wait, L2 simulation, 8K session wave bridge and M4 remain NOT_RUN.
