**Model-free cache discriminator.** `tools/c123_analyze_trace.py` validates the frozen raw hashes and markers, reconstructs calls and interval unions, and simulates a separate completed-load LRU against the fixed observed primary miss stream. At 3 GiB, global LRU would hit 62/1509 decode loads (4.11% logical bytes); GPU-only LRU would hit 64/426 GPU decode loads (15.02% of GPU-load population). It assumes free insertion and does not change the primary schedule, so these are optimistic fixed-stream read-avoidance counts, not speedups. Both are below the previous 20% investment filter in their respective populations. A full L2 or predictor is not justified on this workload by these simple policies. This does not bound every policy, every conversation or a changed primary cache.

**Decision.** Readiness blocking is a large observed component of decode, and read intervals cover most of that period. Prioritize a small discriminant for increasing independent read progress of *already authoritative* expert components, while preserving routing, compute order, memory bounds and completion fences. First measure component read size/latency and effective overlap from this trace and compare a bounded direct-I/O microprobe with the same file/offset pattern at current and increased queue depth; only a material, reproducible gain may justify a backend intervention. Preserve C121 as screen-only and reserve fresh confirmation pairs for the unmodified C75 profile.
