Reopen only an externally identified terminal D0 or D1 receipt. Rehash its exact raw member set (including native stdout/stderr, monitor JSONL and C118 when present), the 17 supplied IDs and the zero, one or two captured logits named in the receipt. Bind the reviewed trace to the sealed supervisor, input and provider authorities. No reading of the model or measurement of the host. The arm's terminal verdict remains authoritative; a stopped or pre-spawn arm can yield partial cost evidence. A missing/tampered required file in a terminal inventory fails the independent review.

On native CLOCK_MONOTONIC, pair load, context, prefill, supplied teacher and cleanup begin/end markers in order. Preserve any open phase as censored. A negative after prefill may skip the teacher and still close cleanup. Adjacent pairs of markers leave gaps for publication/verification. The supervisor's elapsed clock is spawn-to-observed-exit and has no absolute epoch in the saved samples. Expose the native span and supervisor wall separately when present. Do not impute time to process kill, PID disappearance, post-validation, an unfinished phase or a resource phase interval from these different origins.

Resource counters include the supervisor and charged cache, anon/file peaks are individually sampled. For reclaim, major faults, pressure and events show intervals with explicit first-observed and terminal counters; show post-child counters separately. PSI totals are microseconds and avg10 percentages refer to rolling windows. Preserve io.stat's per-device endpoints or endpoint delta as supplied. Compare to the live observed slice where defined; namespace sectors (512 bytes) include concurrent activity. No device totals are added to namespace or attributed to a specific forward phase. Report sample count, span/gaps and individually identified process counters. Power/energy, synchronous resource peaks by component and wall-clock termination when unrecorded remain unknown. A review of an authentic FAIL describes only recorded evidence and never authorizes a subsequent arm. Prospective tests cover interruption before/after prefill, integrity failure and independent io/namespace denominators.
